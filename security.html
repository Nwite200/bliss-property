How passkeys work

Your device keeps the key. Bliss Legacy stores only a public key and basic device metadata. We never receive or store fingerprint images, facial scans, biometric templates, device PINs or private keys.
Recovery stays available. If you lose a device, sign in with your password and remove the lost passkey here. Then add a replacement device. Password reset and account recovery continue to work exactly as before.
Removing a device needs your password. This protects your account if someone briefly gains access to an unlocked session. Renaming a device is low-risk and does not require reauthentication.

The exact authentication method (fingerprint, face, Windows Hello or device PIN) is chosen by your device and browser. Bliss Legacy does not control or record which method you use.
